Privacy policy

health-monitor is a personal app used only by its owner.

Data read from Withings and Google Health is stored only on the owner's own computer (and in the owner's own encrypted backups). It is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

The app uses Google user data only to display the owner's own health metrics to the owner, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

To revoke access, remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
